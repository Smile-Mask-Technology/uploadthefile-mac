# Security

Please report vulnerabilities in this app, the `uptf` command-line tool or the `UptfKit`
library privately to **abuse@uploadthefile.com** (the contact published in
https://uploadthefile.com/.well-known/security.txt), with steps to reproduce. Do not open a
public issue for a vulnerability.

Issues in the upload.tf service itself (the website, the API, hosted pages on `*.upload.tf`)
go to the same address.

To report abuse or illegal content hosted on the service, use
https://uploadthefile.com/report instead (no account needed).

What this app keeps on your Mac, and why it is not sandboxed, is described in
[docs/DEVELOPMENT.md](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md#how-it-works).
